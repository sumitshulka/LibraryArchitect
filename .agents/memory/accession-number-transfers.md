---
name: Accession numbers during transfers
description: User-confirmed behavior when a physical copy moves between libraries with library-scoped accession numbering.
---

When a copy is received by another library under library-scoped numbering, archive its old source accession in transfer history and assign the receiving library's next accession number. The receiving librarian confirms the catalog-card update.

**Why:** the user chose “Archive old; assign new at destination” after clarification.

**How to apply:** do not carry the old library's accession number into the destination. Preserve it as audit history, and make the new number visible on the receiving library's catalog detail.
